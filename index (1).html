Who We Are

Trade intermediation, run like an advisory practice.

Meritria South & Co. operates as an Export/Import Management Company (EMC/ETC), representing producers in markets where they currently have no commercial footprint. We work on a commission basis and never take ownership of the goods we place.

What sets us apart is timing. Instead of waiting for a client to bring us a request, we run a continuous, proprietary screening process across countries and product categories — watching for tariff asymmetries, concentrated supplier bases, and disruptions that open a real, defensible window of opportunity. When we find one, we bring it to the producer.
